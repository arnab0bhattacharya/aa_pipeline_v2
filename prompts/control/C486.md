Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An heiress of twenty-five, who has refused several offers and is thought cold, is unexpectedly courted by her late brother's closest friend, a naval surgeon of no family. Her guardian is opposed. She must decide whether her caution has been wisdom or merely fear.
Setting: A country house near Portsmouth, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
