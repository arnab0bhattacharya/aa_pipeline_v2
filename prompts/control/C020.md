Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A shepherd who has worked forty years on the same farm is told by the new tenant that he is too old. The shepherd's grandson, a quick boy who has learned to read, writes a letter on his behalf to the landlord, who has never visited the farm.
Setting: Downland farm in Wiltshire, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
