Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young wife whose husband is a successful but dull estate agent joins a society for the discussion of advanced ideas. Her husband is first amused, then alarmed. When she announces she wishes to earn her own money, he goes to her father for help.
Setting: A London suburb, 1900s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
