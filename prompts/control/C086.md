Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young man who has failed at every profession decides to become a critic, on the principle that criticism requires no talent. His first review is so cruel and so brilliant that he becomes famous overnight, and every artist in London wants to be insulted by him.
Setting: London, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
