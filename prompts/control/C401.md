Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A brother and sister who have run their late father's draper's shop together for ten years quarrel when the brother wishes to marry a woman the sister thinks shallow. The shop cannot support two households. Each believes the other's happiness depends on their own sacrifice.
Setting: A small town in Lincolnshire, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
