Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A newspaper editor notices that personal advertisements placed in his columns have begun using numbers instead of words. A reader's letter suggests they are messages between thieves. When the editor's typesetter is found beaten in an alley, the editor seeks out a private detective.
Setting: Fleet Street, London, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
