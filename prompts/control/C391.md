Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A ship's carpenter is accused by the crew of stealing from the stores during a long calm. The captain believes him innocent but cannot prove it. As the calm continues and water runs short, the crew's suspicion hardens into something dangerous.
Setting: A sailing ship becalmed in the Atlantic, 1880s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
