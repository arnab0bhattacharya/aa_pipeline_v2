Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A boy who works at a pithead sorting coal is injured in an accident. The mine owner offers his mother five pounds to sign a paper. A young chapel preacher advises her not to sign, and the owner's agent threatens to evict the family.
Setting: A mining village in Durham, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
