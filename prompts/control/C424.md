Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An old weaver's cottage is wanted by the new railway company. The weaver refuses to sell. His neighbours, who have been offered good prices, begin to resent his stubbornness, and the parson is asked to persuade him.
Setting: A village in Warwickshire, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
