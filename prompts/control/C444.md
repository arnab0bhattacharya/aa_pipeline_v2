Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A shy young curate, mistaken for a famous explorer at a society reception, is asked to describe his adventures. He invents them on the spot. By the end of the evening, he has been invited to lead an expedition to Africa.
Setting: A reception in Mayfair, London, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
