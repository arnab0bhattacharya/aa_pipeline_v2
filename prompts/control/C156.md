Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young woman nursing her sick mother is visited daily by the doctor's assistant, a clever, poor young man. Her family assume she will marry the prosperous neighbour who has courted her for years. She must decide how much to let herself feel before the mother recovers or dies.
Setting: A riverside town in Lincolnshire, 1830s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
