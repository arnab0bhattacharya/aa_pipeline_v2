Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A teacher abroad receives news that her small savings, left in an English bank, have been lost in its failure. She tells no one, continues teaching, and calculates how many months she can survive before she must ask the director for an advance she is too proud to request.
Setting: A girls' school in a Continental city, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
