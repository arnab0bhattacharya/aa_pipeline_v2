Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A governess is falsely accused of encouraging the attentions of her employer's son. The son is too cowardly to clear her name. Dismissed without a reference, she sets out on foot towards the nearest town, with a few shillings and her pride intact.
Setting: A country estate in Lincolnshire, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
