[Scenario]
A young woman who has been in service in a great house returns to her parents' cottage with fine manners and a secret. Her mother sees she is unhappy. Her father wants her to marry the blacksmith, who has waited for her for three years.
Setting: A village in Staffordshire, 1800s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
