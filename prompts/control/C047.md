Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young woman travelling with her father in Egypt meets a young archaeologist who is excavating a tomb. She is fascinated by his work, and by him. Her father, who wishes her to marry a cousin at home, decides to cut the journey short.
Setting: Luxor and the Nile, 1900s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
