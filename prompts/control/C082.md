Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young woman's sister has been courted for two years by a gentleman who will not declare himself. The younger sister, impatient, resolves to make him speak. Her methods are ingenious, and very nearly drive him away.
Setting: A village in Hampshire, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
