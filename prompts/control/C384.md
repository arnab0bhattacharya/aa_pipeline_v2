Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An old man who has outlived his wife, his children, and all his friends lives alone in a decaying manor. A young tenant farmer's daughter begins to visit him, and he finds himself telling her the story of his life, which no one has ever heard.
Setting: A manor in Cumberland, 1830s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
