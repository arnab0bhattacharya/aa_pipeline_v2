Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A clergyman's daughter reports that her father returned from a parish visit pale and silent, and has since refused to preach. Every night he sits writing a letter that he burns at dawn. She begs an investigator to learn what happened in a poor street by the river.
Setting: A parish in Southwark, London, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
