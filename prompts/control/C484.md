Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A fashionable dressmaker finds that customers' measurements have been stolen from her books, and soon afterwards several clients are robbed at private balls. The thief seems to know exactly which gowns conceal jewelled ornaments. She consults a detective rather than lose her reputation.
Setting: Regent Street, London, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
