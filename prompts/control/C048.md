Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A retired colonel spends a day in his London club, reading the newspapers, eating his lunch, and talking to no one. In the afternoon he receives a telegram announcing that his son is getting married. He does not know the bride.
Setting: A club in St James's, London, 1920s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
