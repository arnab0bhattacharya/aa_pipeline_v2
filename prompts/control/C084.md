Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young man raised by a stern aunt who keeps a lodging house discovers that his father, believed dead, is alive and working as a ticket collector at a provincial railway station. He goes to see him without announcing himself.
Setting: London and a railway town in the Midlands, 1850s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
