Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young woman cataloguing the library of a great house for its new owner finds the house's history written in the margins of its books: a girl's pencilled verses, a soldier's notes, a mother's list of children's illnesses. She begins to feel she knows the family better than its heir does.
Setting: A great house in Northamptonshire, 1910s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
