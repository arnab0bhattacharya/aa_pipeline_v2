Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young lady staying in London for the season is introduced to a gentleman whose name she recognises as that of a man her brother quarrelled with years before. She does not know the cause of the quarrel. He clearly does.
Setting: London, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
