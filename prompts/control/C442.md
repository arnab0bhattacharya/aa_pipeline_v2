Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A village wheelwright, respected for his honesty, is asked by the squire's steward to value a neighbour's cart for a debt seizure. He knows the true value will ruin the neighbour, and that a lower figure would be a lie. The whole village waits to see what he will say.
Setting: A village in Warwickshire, 1800s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
