Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young woman who has been engaged for a year to a dull and kind man breaks the engagement on the evening before a family dinner. She must sit through the dinner with her parents, her aunts, and her former fiancé, who has not been told.
Setting: A house in Kensington, London, 1910s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
