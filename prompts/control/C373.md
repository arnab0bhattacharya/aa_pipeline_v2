Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An Englishwoman teaching abroad is courted, half in jest, by a wealthy English traveller passing through the city. He offers to take her home to England as his wife. She suspects he is bored, and fears that she is only lonely.
Setting: A Continental city and its English hotel, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
