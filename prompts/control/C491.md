Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An astronomer who has devoted his life to mapping the heavens loses his sight. His daughter, who has assisted him for years, offers to continue the work. He refuses to believe a woman capable of it, and she must prove him wrong without breaking his heart.
Setting: An observatory on a hill in Surrey, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
