Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young man recovering from an illness is sent by his doctors to a hotel in the mountains of Switzerland. Among the invalids and their families he meets a young woman who is not ill at all, but who has come to escape her family.
Setting: A hotel in the Swiss Alps, 1900s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
