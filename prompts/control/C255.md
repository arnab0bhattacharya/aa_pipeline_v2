Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A captain who has spent his life on sailing ships is given command of a steamer. He distrusts the engines and the engineers. When the engines fail in a storm, he must rely on the men he has despised.
Setting: A steamer in the Bay of Biscay, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
