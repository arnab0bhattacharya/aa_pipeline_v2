Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A golden fish in a palace fountain is granted the power of speech by a fairy. It uses its gift to tell the courtiers the truth about one another. The court is thrown into confusion, and the king must decide whether to silence the fish or to listen.
Setting: A palace courtyard in a southern kingdom

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
