Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A customs officer in a South American port is offered a fortune to look away while a ship is loaded at night. He has been honest all his life and poor all his life. His wife is ill, and the ship sails at dawn.
Setting: A South American port, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
