Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young man working as a clerk for a firm of solicitors is asked to deliver a packet to a lady in a great house. She receives it without opening it, and asks him to call again. He returns every week for a year without learning what the packet contained.
Setting: London and a great house in Essex, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
