Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A woman who has worked for thirty years as a housekeeper in a great house is remembered, after her death, by the family she served, the servants she ruled, and the village where she was born. Each remembers a different woman.
Setting: A great house in Sussex, 1890s to 1920s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
