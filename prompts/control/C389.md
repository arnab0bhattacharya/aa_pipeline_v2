Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A labourer's widow who has always been too proud to accept parish relief falls ill in winter. The rector's wife, who has quarrelled with her, is the only person who notices. Their reconciliation over the sickbed is grudging and complete.
Setting: A village in the Fens, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
