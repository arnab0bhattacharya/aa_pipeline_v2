Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A lady who has kept a diary of every compliment she has ever received loses it at a garden party. Every guest is terrified that their compliment will be read aloud. The young man who finds it is the only guest who never paid her one.
Setting: A garden party in Richmond, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
