Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A governess is asked by her pupil's mother to keep a diary of the child's progress, and finds that the mother reads it in secret for hints of her husband's movements. The governess begins to write the diary for two readers.
Setting: A house near York, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
