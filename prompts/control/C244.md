Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young woman who sells eggs and cheese at the market is courted by a well-to-do grazier and a poor but clever stone-mason. Her mother favours the grazier. The girl's choice is complicated by a promise she made to the mason before the grazier's interest became known.
Setting: A market village in Nottinghamshire, 1800s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
