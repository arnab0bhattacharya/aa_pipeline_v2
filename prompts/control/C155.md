Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young man about to enter the church is offered a better living by a patron on condition that he marry the patron's plain niece. He has an understanding with a girl of no fortune. His mother urges prudence. He must choose between the living and the girl.
Setting: A parsonage in Gloucestershire, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
