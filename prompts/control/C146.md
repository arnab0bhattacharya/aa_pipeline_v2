Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A boy sent to work in a soap boilery after his father's bankruptcy pretends to his workmates that he is the son of a sea captain lost at sea. He tells the story so well that he begins to believe it, until an old sailor who drinks at the corner pub asks for the captain's name.
Setting: A riverside works near Battersea, London, 1830s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
