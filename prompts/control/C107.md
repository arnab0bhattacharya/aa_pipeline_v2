Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A stonemason working on the restoration of a village church refuses to carve a memorial praising a dead squire whom he knew to be a cruel landlord. The squire's widow is paying. The vicar asks the mason whether truth belongs on gravestones.
Setting: A village in Staffordshire, 1800s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
