[Scenario]
A Russian student accused by the exiles of being a police spy must prove his innocence to a committee that has already decided his guilt. The only witness who could clear him is the man who denounced him.
Setting: A lodging house in Geneva, 1900s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
