Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young man who has always believed himself the son of a poor curate learns that he is the illegitimate son of a nobleman. The nobleman, now dying, wishes to acknowledge him. The curate, who raised him, does not wish to lose him.
Setting: A parsonage and a great house in Northumberland, 1830s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
