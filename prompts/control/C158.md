Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A boy who has always been his father's favourite is accused at school of cheating, and his elder sister, who knows he is guilty, is asked by their father to speak for his character. Her loyalty to her brother and her truthfulness cannot both survive.
Setting: A village in Lincolnshire, 1820s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
