Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
In a future where disease has been abolished, a young man becomes the first person in a century to fall ill. The society, which has no doctors and no hospitals, does not know how to treat him, and fears him.
Setting: England in the far future

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
