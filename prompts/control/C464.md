Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young woman who works in a bookshop is courted by a wealthy customer who knows nothing about books. She is tempted by his kindness and his money. Her friend, a struggling poet, tells her she would be selling herself.
Setting: A bookshop in Charing Cross Road, London, 1910s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
