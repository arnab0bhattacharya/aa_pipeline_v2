Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A man is found dead in an empty house with an American newspaper folded beside him, a single date ringed in ink. Scotland Yard is puzzled, but a private investigator recognises the newspaper's town as the site of a notorious bank robbery twenty years before.
Setting: An empty house in Lambeth, London, 1880s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
