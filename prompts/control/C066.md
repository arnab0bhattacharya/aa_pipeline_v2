Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A parish clerk who prides himself on economy is put in charge of distributing coal to the poor in a hard winter. His own elderly mother, whom he has not visited in years, turns out to be on the list.
Setting: A London parish in winter, 1830s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
