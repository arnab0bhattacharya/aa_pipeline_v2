Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A ship's boy who survived a wreck in which the captain went down with the ship is asked by the captain's widow to tell her how her husband died. The boy knows the captain died a coward. He must decide what to tell her.
Setting: A port town in Wales, 1880s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
