Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young woman's father arranges her marriage to a prosperous merchant twenty years her senior, who is generous and dull. She feels no affection for him, but her family's debts are pressing. On the eve of the wedding she must decide whether gratitude is enough to build a life upon.
Setting: A village in the Pennines, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
