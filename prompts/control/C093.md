Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young man has promised his dying mother that he will look after his weak-minded brother. When an opportunity arrives to emigrate to Canada and make his fortune, the brother cannot come. He must decide whether a promise to the dead binds the living.
Setting: A market town in Nottinghamshire, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
