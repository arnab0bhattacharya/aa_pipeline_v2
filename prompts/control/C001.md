Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young heiress whose father was killed in a duel learns that the man who killed him is now a respected member of Parliament, and is about to marry her closest friend. She has proof of the duel. She must decide whether to speak.
Setting: London, 1820s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
