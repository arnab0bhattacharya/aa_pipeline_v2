Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A lecturer in physics accidentally creates a tiny pocket of space where the laws of nature are reversed. He keeps it in a glass jar in his rooms, and his landlady, cleaning, knocks it over.
Setting: Lodgings in Oxford, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
