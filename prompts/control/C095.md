Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A widow who keeps a respectable lodging house is visited by a stranger who knows that she was once married under another name, to a man who was transported for forgery. He asks for money. She must decide whether her present life is worth what he demands.
Setting: A lodging house in Bath, 1830s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
