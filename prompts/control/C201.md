Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A respectable doctor's widow takes in a lodger, a mysterious foreign gentleman who pays well and keeps strange hours. The town's gossip fastens on the household. The widow's daughter, who has nothing else to do, begins to investigate the lodger herself.
Setting: A provincial town in the Midlands, 1830s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
