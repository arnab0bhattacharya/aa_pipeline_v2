Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A harbour pilot who once refused to take a ship out in a storm, against the owner's demand, learns years later that the owner has become chairman of the harbour board. The pilot is summoned to the board to explain a minor delay.
Setting: A harbour in Liverpool, 1880s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
