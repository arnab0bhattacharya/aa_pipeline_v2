Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A professor who has discovered a substance that cancels the effect of gravity tests it on a garden chair. The chair floats away. The professor's housekeeper, who sat on it, is carried over the rooftops of a quiet town.
Setting: A university town in the Midlands, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
