Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A dealer in old rags and bones, a miser by reputation, dies in his yard and is discovered to have hidden money in every heap. His neighbours descend on the yard. A young servant girl who once nursed him knows where the largest sum is hidden.
Setting: A rag-and-bone yard in Clerkenwell, London, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
