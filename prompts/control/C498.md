Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A small community survives in the ruins of a great city after a pestilence. Its leader, a former soldier, wishes to rebuild; his rival, a former priest, believes the pestilence was a judgement and that rebuilding is blasphemy. The young people of the community must choose between them.
Setting: The ruins of a European city, late in the twenty-first century

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
