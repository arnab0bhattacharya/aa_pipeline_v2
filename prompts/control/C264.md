Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A traveller in a future world is taken for a ghost by a people who have never seen a man with grey hair. Their society has found a way to halt ageing at thirty, and they regard his age as a deformity. He tries to explain the dignity of growing old.
Setting: A city in the far future

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
