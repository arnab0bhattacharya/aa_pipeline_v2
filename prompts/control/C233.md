Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
Two young women from a country parish attend their first public ball at a resort town. One dances every dance; the other dances none. The one who dances none is the one who receives a call the next morning.
Setting: Weymouth, 1800s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
