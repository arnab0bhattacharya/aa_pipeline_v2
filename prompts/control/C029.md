Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young man from the lower middle classes wins a scholarship to a technical college and meets students of every class. He is ashamed of his origins and ashamed of his shame. When his father visits the college, he must decide whether to acknowledge him.
Setting: South Kensington, London, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
