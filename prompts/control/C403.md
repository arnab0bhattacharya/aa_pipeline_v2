Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A doctor who treats the poor of the East End is consulted secretly by an anarchist who has been injured making a bomb. The doctor's oath obliges him to treat the man. His conscience demands that he inform the police.
Setting: Whitechapel, London, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
