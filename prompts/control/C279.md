Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An engineer building a new railway viaduct finds his surveying stakes moved each night, always to divert the line away from a particular farm. The farmer protests innocence, and the railway company threatens to dismiss the engineer, who engages an investigator.
Setting: A valley in Yorkshire, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
