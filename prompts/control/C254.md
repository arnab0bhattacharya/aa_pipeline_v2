Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A widow who lost her only son at the Somme goes each morning to feed the ducks in a London park, at the hour he used to walk there before the war. A boy of about the same age begins to sit on the next bench, and she cannot decide whether she wants him to speak.
Setting: St James's Park, London, 1920s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
