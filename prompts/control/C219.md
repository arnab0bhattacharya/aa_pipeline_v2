Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A clergyman's wife who delights in arranging her parishioners' lives promotes a match between the village doctor and the squire's niece. Both are polite to her and to each other. Neither has the slightest wish to marry, and they unite only in their efforts to escape her kindness.
Setting: A village in Berkshire, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
