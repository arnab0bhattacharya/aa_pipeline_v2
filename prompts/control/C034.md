Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An English teacher abroad attends a lecture by a famous orator, and at the door meets a fellow countryman she once rejected as a suitor. He is now prosperous and married. They speak for ten minutes in the cold, and she walks home alone.
Setting: A Continental city in winter, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
