Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young widow with a small fortune is pursued by a charming fortune hunter whom her friends warn her against. She marries him to prove her independence of judgement. Within a year she must decide whether to admit her friends were right.
Setting: A spa town and London, 1860s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
