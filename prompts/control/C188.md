Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A king who has never laughed offers half his kingdom to anyone who can make him smile. Jesters, poets, and philosophers fail. A small kitchen maid who has never heard of the offer drops a tray of tarts in front of him.
Setting: A kingdom of gardens and fountains

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
