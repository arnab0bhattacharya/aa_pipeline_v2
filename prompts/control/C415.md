Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
In a future England slowly recovering from a pestilence, a woman who lost her children to the disease is asked to take in an orphan. She refuses. The orphan, a silent boy of six, keeps returning to her door.
Setting: A village in Berkshire, late in the twenty-first century

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
