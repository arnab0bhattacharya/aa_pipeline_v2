Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young woman learns that her closest friend's brother, whom she has privately liked for some time, has lost his money at cards and is leaving for the West Indies. Her friend begs her to say nothing that might keep him in England, where his creditors wait. She must hold her tongue at the farewell dinner.
Setting: A country house in Sussex, 1800s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
