Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A doctor on a remote trading station in Africa is visited by an English scientist who wishes to study a local disease that confers immunity to pain. The scientist's methods trouble the doctor, who begins to fear for the villagers.
Setting: A trading station on the West African coast, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
