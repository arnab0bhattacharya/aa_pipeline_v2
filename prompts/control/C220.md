Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A night-school teacher in a manufacturing town is told by the mill owners who fund the school that he must teach only arithmetic and the catechism. His best pupil, a grown man who works the boilers, asks to be taught to read poetry.
Setting: A manufacturing town in the north, 1850s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
