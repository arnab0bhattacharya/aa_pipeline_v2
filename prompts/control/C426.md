Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A fisherman's widow on a coast of a turbulent republic finds a drowned soldier on the beach with a satchel of letters. Some are addressed to the government, some to the rebels. She cannot read, and must decide whom to trust to read them for her.
Setting: A fishing village on a South American coast, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
