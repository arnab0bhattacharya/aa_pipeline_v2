Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An outbreak of madness among the horses of London makes the streets impassable. A cab driver who has always loved his horse refuses to have it shot, and hides it in a basement while the city falls into chaos above.
Setting: London, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
