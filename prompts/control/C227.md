Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
In a future England governed as a republic, a young statesman who has dedicated his life to the public good is told that a famine in the colonies will reach the home country within a year. His colleagues prefer to hope it will not. He must decide whether to tell the people.
Setting: London, late in the twenty-first century

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
