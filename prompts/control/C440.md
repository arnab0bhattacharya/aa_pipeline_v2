Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young Englishwoman returns from her father's sheep station to claim a London inheritance, and is met at the dock by a solicitor she does not know. Within days she suspects the man is not a solicitor at all, and writes in secret to a detective.
Setting: London, with a past in New Zealand, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
