Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A woman who teaches evening classes for working men falls in love with one of her students, a printer with a wife and children. She tells no one and continues to teach him. He brings her a book he has printed himself, and she does not know what he means by it.
Setting: A working men's college in Camden, London, 1910s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
