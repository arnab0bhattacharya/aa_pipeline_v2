Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young woman whose brother has been arrested as an anarchist goes to the police to plead for him, and finds the inspector both courteous and immovable. She returns the next day, and the next. Something in her persistence troubles the inspector.
Setting: Scotland Yard, London, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
