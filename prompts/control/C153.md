Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A boy of ten is sent to live with a grandfather he has never met, a retired pawnbroker who lives above his shuttered shop and trusts nobody. The boy must earn the old man's affection while discovering why every pledge in the shop has been kept for thirty years.
Setting: A narrow street near Holborn, London, 1830s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
