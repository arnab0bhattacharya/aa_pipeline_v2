Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An English teacher at a foreign school receives a letter from home informing her that her only brother has died. No one at the school knows her well enough to comfort her. She must continue teaching the next morning, and the effort of composure becomes the story.
Setting: A girls' school in a Continental city, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
