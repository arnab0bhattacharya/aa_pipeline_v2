Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
Two sisters are invited to stay with a cousin who has married into a great family. They are treated as poor relations. The younger sister is resentful; the elder observes that the cousin is not happy either.
Setting: A great house in London, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
