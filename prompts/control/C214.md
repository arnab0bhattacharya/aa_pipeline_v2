Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A harbour master in a small Eastern port is ordered by the colonial government to refuse entry to a ship flying no flag. Its captain sends ashore a letter in a hand the harbour master recognises: his own brother's, whom he has not seen in twenty years.
Setting: A port in the Dutch East Indies, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
