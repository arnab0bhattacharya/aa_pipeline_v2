Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A rector who preaches obedience to the poor is challenged in the vestry by a self-taught weaver who quotes scripture back at him. The rector's daughter, who overhears, begins secretly reading the books the weaver recommends.
Setting: A moorland parish above a mill valley, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
