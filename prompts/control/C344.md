Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A brilliant young man who has failed to win a university prize resolves to abandon scholarship for a life of travel. His guardian, who sacrificed much to educate him, receives the news without reproach, and the young man finds that silence harder than anger.
Setting: Cambridge and a country rectory, 1860s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
