Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A cooper in a brewery town discovers his master has been watering the ale sold to the workhouse. He is the only man who knows. Speaking up will cost his place and his children's bread, and staying silent will cost him something he cannot name.
Setting: A brewery town in Kent, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
