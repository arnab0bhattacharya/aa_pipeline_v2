Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young woman agrees to marry an older man for his kindness and his estate, and on the eve of her wedding meets his nephew, who is everything she had once hoped for. She does not break her promise. The nephew does not ask her to.
Setting: An estate in Herefordshire, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
