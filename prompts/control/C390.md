Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An art student lodging above a jeweller's shop hears hammering through the floor every night, though the shop is closed. The jeweller tells her he repairs clocks after hours. When the hammering stops and the jeweller disappears, she reports to an investigator.
Setting: Hatton Garden, London, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
