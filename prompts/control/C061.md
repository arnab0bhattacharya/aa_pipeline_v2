Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A farmer's son who has been away as a soldier returns to find his sweetheart married to his brother. He takes work on the farm as a labourer and lives in the same house. All three must find a way to live together that does not end in ruin.
Setting: A farm in Derbyshire after the wars, 1816

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
