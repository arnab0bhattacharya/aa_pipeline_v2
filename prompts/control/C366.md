Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An English teacher at a foreign school is asked to supervise the director's own daughter, a clever, deceitful girl of fifteen. The girl tests every rule and reports every lapse to her mother. The teacher must manage her without either cruelty or surrender.
Setting: A girls' school in a Flemish city, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
