Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A mill owner's ward, rich and impulsive, decides to manage her own estate and tenants. The local gentry are scandalised, the parson amused, and her own uncle obstructs her at every turn. She learns that independence in a woman is regarded as a kind of madness.
Setting: A manor in a Yorkshire manufacturing valley, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
