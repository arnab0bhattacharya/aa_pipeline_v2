Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An English teacher abroad is visited by an Englishwoman who claims to be a distant cousin and seems to know a great deal about the teacher's family. The cousin is charming and asks for a loan. The teacher, starved of home, wants to believe her.
Setting: A Continental capital, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
