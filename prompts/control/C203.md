Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young man who has come to London from the provinces to make his name as a writer spends a day walking the city, looking for a room, a job, and something to write about. By evening he has found none of them, and has seen everything.
Setting: London, 1920s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
