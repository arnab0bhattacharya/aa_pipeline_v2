Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An old lady who has been the centre of a famous literary circle is visited by a young journalist who wishes to write about her. She tells him stories that are not quite true, and watches him believe them.
Setting: A house in Chelsea, London, 1910s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
