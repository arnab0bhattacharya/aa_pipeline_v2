Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A clergyman's family is visited by a wealthy cousin who means to choose a bride among the daughters. The youngest daughter, who is fifteen and outspoken, makes it her business to mock him. Her sisters are horrified; the cousin is unexpectedly charmed.
Setting: A parsonage in Northamptonshire, 1800s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
