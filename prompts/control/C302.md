Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A woman who has lived in the same London street for forty years walks its length on the day before the house is to be sold. She remembers the people who lived in each house, and the people she was when she knew them.
Setting: A street in Bloomsbury, London, 1920s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
