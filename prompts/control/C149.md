Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An English railway engineer surveying a line through the mountains of a small republic is caught between the government that hired him and a rebel general who controls the passes. Both demand his loyalty, and each promises that the railway will bring civilisation.
Setting: A South American republic, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
