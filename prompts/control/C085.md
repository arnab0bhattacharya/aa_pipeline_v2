Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young woman who is certain that a neighbour's governess is designing to marry her employer sets out to protect him. The governess has no such design. The employer, however, has.
Setting: A country house in Hampshire, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
