Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A sensible young woman is mortified when her mother announces at a dinner party that her daughter will soon be engaged to a gentleman who has never proposed. The gentleman is present. The daughter must undo the damage without contradicting her mother in public.
Setting: A house in a country town in Hertfordshire, 1800s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
