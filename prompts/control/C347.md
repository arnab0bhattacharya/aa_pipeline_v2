Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A surveyor sent to map a river for a concession company finds that the villages marked on his predecessor's chart do not exist, and never did. The company's directors in Europe have already sold shares on the strength of the chart. He must decide what his own map will show.
Setting: A river in Central Africa, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
