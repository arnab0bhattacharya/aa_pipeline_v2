Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An elderly baronet who has always claimed descent from a medieval king is presented with a genealogy showing that he is descended from the king's cook. His wife is devastated. He finds it the most interesting thing that has ever happened to him.
Setting: A country house in Wiltshire, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
