Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A gentleman of good family but small income is pressed by his sisters to marry a rich widow they have chosen for him. He likes the widow and dislikes being managed. He must decide whether to marry her in spite of his sisters or refuse her because of them.
Setting: A country house in Berkshire, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
