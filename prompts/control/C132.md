Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A scholar of the future, living in a world that has survived a great pestilence, writes a history of the catastrophe for those who will come after. As he writes, he realises that the pestilence has returned.
Setting: An English university town, late in the twenty-first century

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
