Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
Two brothers inherit a small grocery shop. The elder runs it with care while the younger dreams and borrows. When the younger is engaged to a rich man's daughter, he asks the elder to sell the shop to pay for the wedding.
Setting: A high street in Chatham, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
