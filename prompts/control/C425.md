Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young officer returning from the Peninsular War discovers that his late father's fortune was made in the slave trade. He is engaged to a woman of strong abolitionist views. He must decide whether to tell her before the wedding.
Setting: A country house in Gloucestershire, 1814

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
