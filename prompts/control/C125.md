Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A fire destroys a mill in a manufacturing town, throwing three hundred hands out of work. The owner, insured, plans to rebuild elsewhere. A foreman who saved the ledgers from the fire discovers that the insurance was taken out only a week before.
Setting: A manufacturing town in Yorkshire, 1850s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
