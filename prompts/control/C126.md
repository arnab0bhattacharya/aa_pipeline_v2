Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A girl raised by a reclusive man she believes to be her uncle finds a miniature portrait of a woman who looks exactly like her. He tells her the woman was his sister. The local vicar, who knew the family, tells her something else.
Setting: A remote house on the Welsh coast, 1830s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
