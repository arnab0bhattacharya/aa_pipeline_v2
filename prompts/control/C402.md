Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A schoolteacher's sister, a quiet invalid, writes poems she shows no one. When a visiting critic praises them by chance, the elder sister, who has given up her own ambitions to care for the family, is torn between pride and an ugly jealousy she is ashamed to feel.
Setting: A parsonage in the Pennines, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
