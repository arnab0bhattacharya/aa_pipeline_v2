Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young man who has always been expected to take orders loses his faith while preparing for his ordination. His family's income depends on the living he is to receive. He must tell his mother, who has prayed for this day for twenty years.
Setting: A provincial town and a nearby rectory, 1830s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
