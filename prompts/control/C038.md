Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A bookseller finds that rare volumes returned by a regular customer have had single pages removed with a razor. The missing pages share nothing obvious, but each contains a map. When the customer stops visiting, the bookseller asks an investigator to discover what he is assembling.
Setting: Charing Cross Road, London, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
