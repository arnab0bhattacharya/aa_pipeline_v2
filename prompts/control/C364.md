Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A woman's friendships with three other women, from school to old age, are seen in a series of meetings: a tea party, a wedding, a funeral, a chance encounter in a railway station. Each meeting changes what the others mean.
Setting: London and the country, 1890s to 1920s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
