Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A respectable spinster who has lent money to half the town is found to be nearly ruined by a bad investment. Her debtors, relieved of their obligations by her silence, must decide whether to repay her now that she needs it.
Setting: A cathedral town in the Midlands, 1830s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
