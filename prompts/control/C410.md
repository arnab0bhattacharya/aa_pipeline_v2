Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A girl of sixteen whose father has failed in business is sent to keep house for a bachelor uncle, a dry and silent lawyer. She expects to be miserable. Over a winter she finds that the uncle reads poetry in secret and has been unhappy for thirty years.
Setting: A cathedral town in the Midlands, 1820s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
