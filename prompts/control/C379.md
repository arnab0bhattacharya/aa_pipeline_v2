Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young widow whose husband drowned within a year of their marriage retires to a cottage by the sea. She refuses all company. A shy neighbour, a girl of fifteen who also lost a parent to the sea, begins leaving wild flowers at her door.
Setting: A cottage on the Devon coast, 1820s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
