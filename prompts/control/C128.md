Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A girl whose closest friend has died of consumption spends a summer writing letters to the dead friend, as if she still lived. Her mother finds the letters and fears for her daughter's mind.
Setting: A village in Devon, 1820s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
