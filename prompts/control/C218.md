Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young wife discovers that her husband, a respectable wine merchant, has two sets of account books and a second house key she cannot match to any door. Fearing scandal more than the truth, she hires an investigator to find out where the key belongs.
Setting: Hampstead and central London, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
