Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A naval officer on shore leave escorts his sister to Bath, where she meets a gentleman he despises. He must decide whether to forbid the acquaintance, knowing that his sister is of age and he has no right to command her.
Setting: Bath, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
