Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A respectable family discovers that their butler is the author of a scandalous novel that has become the talk of London. They cannot dismiss him without admitting they have read it, and they cannot keep him without reading the sequel.
Setting: A house in Kensington, London, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
