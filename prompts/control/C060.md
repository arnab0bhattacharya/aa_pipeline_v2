Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A woman travelling by train to her sister's funeral finds herself sharing a compartment with a young soldier going home on leave. They speak only of the weather and the countryside, and by the end of the journey each knows something about the other that neither has told anyone.
Setting: A railway carriage between London and the north, 1916

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
