Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young man returns to his native village after making a fortune in Australia and finds his old friends either dead or ashamed of him. Only the village schoolmistress, who taught him to read, receives him as he was.
Setting: A village in Suffolk, 1850s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
