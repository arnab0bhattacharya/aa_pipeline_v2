Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young Englishwoman who has become the most respected teacher at a foreign school is offered a post in England at higher pay. Her pupils beg her to stay, and so, wordlessly, does a colleague she has never quite befriended.
Setting: A girls' school in a Flemish city, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
