Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An elderly duchess who has outlived three husbands decides to write her memoirs. Every family in society is terrified. Her nephew, who stands to inherit, is commissioned to edit the manuscript, and discovers that its most scandalous pages concern his own mother.
Setting: A town house in Belgravia, London, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
