Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
Two sisters inherit a small house and an annuity from their godmother. The elder wishes to live quietly; the younger wishes to spend the annuity on a season in London. Their brother, who expected the inheritance himself, offers to mediate.
Setting: A cottage in Dorset, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
