Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A mill hand's wife, worn out by work and grief, is offered a place as housekeeper to the mill owner's widowed brother. Her husband forbids it out of pride. Their children are hungry, and the brother is kind.
Setting: A cotton town in Lancashire, 1850s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
