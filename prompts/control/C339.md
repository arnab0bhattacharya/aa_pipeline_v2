Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young woman who has always laughed at her elder brother's choice of friends finds that the one she mocked most has become a respected magistrate and is now courting her particular friend. She must decide whether to confess to her friend everything she has said of him.
Setting: A village in Surrey, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
