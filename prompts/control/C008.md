Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A naturalist sails to Iceland to study its volcanoes, accompanied by his young wife, who has insisted on coming. On the voyage she proves the better observer. He must decide whether to put her name beside his on the paper he will read to the Royal Society.
Setting: Iceland and a ship in the North Atlantic, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
