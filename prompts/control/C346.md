Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An elderly woman who has lived a life of quiet virtue in a provincial town confesses on her deathbed to her doctor that she once betrayed her closest friend to a man who ruined her. She asks the doctor to find the friend's descendants and ask their forgiveness.
Setting: A provincial town in the Midlands, 1830s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
