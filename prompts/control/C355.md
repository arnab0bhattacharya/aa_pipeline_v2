Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A widowed washerwoman with five children takes in a half-starved boy found sleeping in her copper. Her neighbours call her a fool, and the parish beadle wants the boy returned to the workhouse. She hides him and swears he is her nephew.
Setting: A court off Seven Dials, London, 1830s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
