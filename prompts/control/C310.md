Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A copying clerk who has worked thirty years for the same firm is told he will be replaced by a younger man. He has never missed a day, and his only pleasure is a canary. He goes home to tell his sister, and cannot bring himself to say it.
Setting: Lincoln's Inn Fields and Pentonville, London, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
