Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An estate steward discovers that the lake on his employer's property is being drained at night by someone who refills it before morning. His employer refuses to involve the police. The steward calls in an investigator, who learns that an old chapel once stood where the water now lies.
Setting: A country estate in Shropshire, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
