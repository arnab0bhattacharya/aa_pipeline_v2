Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A cheerful auctioneer who knows everyone's business is asked to sell the effects of an old family fallen on hard times. Among the lots is a packet of letters that would ruin a respected man. He must decide whether to sell, burn, or return them.
Setting: A market town in the Midlands, 1830s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
