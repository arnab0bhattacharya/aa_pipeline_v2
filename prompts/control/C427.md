Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A proud princess who has refused every suitor announces she will marry the man who can bring her something she has never seen. Kings bring jewels and wise men bring wonders. A poor shepherd boy brings her own reflection in a pool, and is thrown out of the palace.
Setting: A kingdom of marble palaces beside a great forest

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
