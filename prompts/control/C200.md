Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young lady who takes pride in her frankness offends the neighbourhood by speaking her mind about a newly married couple. She discovers too late that the bride is the sister of the man she most wishes to please.
Setting: A country town in Kent, 1800s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
