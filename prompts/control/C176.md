Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A pious draper who sits on the board of the town's charity school discovers that its master beats the children. The master is the son of the minister to whom the draper owes his position in the chapel. He must decide whether to speak.
Setting: A provincial town, 1830s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
