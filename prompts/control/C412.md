[Scenario]
A police inspector who has spent years cultivating informers among London's foreign revolutionaries is ordered by a new superior to make an arrest that will please the newspapers. The only man he can plausibly arrest is the informer who has kept him safe.
Setting: Soho and Scotland Yard, London, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
