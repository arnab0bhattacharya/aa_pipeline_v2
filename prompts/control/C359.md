Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young farm labourer who has taught himself to read becomes the leader of a reading society that meets in a barn. The farmer who owns the barn is willing until the society begins to read newspapers. The labourer must choose between his patron and his friends.
Setting: A village in Derbyshire, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
