Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young man who has led a life of pleasure is told by his doctor that he has a year to live. He resolves to spend it doing good, and finds that good deeds are far more difficult than bad ones. The doctor's diagnosis proves to be mistaken.
Setting: London and the Riviera, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
