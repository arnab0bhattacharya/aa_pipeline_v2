Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A gentleman's only son returns from university with debts his father cannot pay. The father proposes to sell the family's best timber. His wife proposes the son marry an heiress. The son proposes nothing, and goes shooting.
Setting: An estate in Lincolnshire, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
