Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A middle-aged politician walks from his club to the House of Commons on the morning of an important debate. He thinks of his speech, of his wife, of a woman he loved in his youth, and of whether any of it matters.
Setting: Pall Mall and Westminster, London, 1920s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
