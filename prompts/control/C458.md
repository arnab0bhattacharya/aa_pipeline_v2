Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A respected banker discovers that his wife, before their marriage, gave birth to a child she gave away. The child, now grown, has come to the town and is asking questions. The banker must decide whether to help his wife or protect his name.
Setting: A provincial town in Yorkshire, 1830s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
