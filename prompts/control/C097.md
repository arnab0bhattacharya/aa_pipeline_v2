Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A shepherd finds a silver bell in a field after a storm. When he rings it, every animal on the hillside kneels. The lord of the valley demands the bell. The shepherd must decide whether to give it up, or to ring it in the lord's hall.
Setting: A valley at the edge of a great plain

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
