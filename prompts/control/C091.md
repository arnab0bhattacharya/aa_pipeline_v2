Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A Russian student living in exile in a Swiss city is approached by a revolutionary committee who believe him to be one of them. He is not, but their trust is the only warmth he has known since leaving home. He must decide how long he can let the mistake continue.
Setting: Geneva, 1900s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
