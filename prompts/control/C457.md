Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A daughter discovers that her mother, long believed a widow, was in fact deserted by a husband who still lives. The father writes asking to meet his daughter before he dies. Her mother forbids it.
Setting: A town in Lincolnshire, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
