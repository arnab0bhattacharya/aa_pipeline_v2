Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A Basque sea captain who carries mail along the coast of a turbulent republic is ordered by a rebel commander to land a cargo of rifles at a fishing village. He has carried every kind of cargo for thirty years and never asked what was in a crate. This time he knows.
Setting: A coastal steamer off a South American republic, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
