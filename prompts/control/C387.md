Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A seaman who gave false evidence at an inquiry to protect his captain finds, years later, that the captain has died and left him a small legacy in gratitude. He must decide whether he can accept money that feels like payment for a lie.
Setting: A port town in Cornwall, 1880s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
