Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An English banker's clerk is sent to France to recover deposits belonging to a noble family who have fled the country. He finds the family's steward running their chateau as his own, and the villagers hungry and watchful. The clerk must decide whose property he is really protecting.
Setting: A chateau in northern France, 1789

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
