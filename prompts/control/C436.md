Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young poet who has published a volume of decadent verse is invited to stay at a country house by a lady who admires his work. Her husband, a hunting squire, does not. Over a weekend the poet must survive the shooting, the dinners, and the husband.
Setting: A country house in Leicestershire, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
