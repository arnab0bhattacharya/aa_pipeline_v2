Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young baronet famous for never having done anything useful is informed by his solicitor that he must earn his own living for one year or forfeit his inheritance. His friends treat the condition as the most amusing scandal of the season. He accepts a post as private secretary to a cabinet minister's wife.
Setting: Mayfair and Westminster, London, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
