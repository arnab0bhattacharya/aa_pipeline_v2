Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A widowed mother and her grown daughter take in lodgers to survive after a bank failure. One lodger, a reserved schoolmaster, begins helping the daughter with her studies. The mother suspects a romance; the daughter wants only to qualify as a teacher and escape the house.
Setting: Leeds, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
