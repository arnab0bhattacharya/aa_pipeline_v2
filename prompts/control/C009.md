Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A manufacturer who has lost everything in a failed speculation proposes to a wealthy woman he does not love. She accepts, then discovers his motive. The story turns on whether she will release him in scorn or hold him to his word.
Setting: A mill town in the West Riding, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
