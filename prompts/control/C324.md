Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young lady newly come into a small fortune is advised by her married sister on every particular of dress, company, and conduct. She follows the advice dutifully for a season, finds herself universally approved and thoroughly bored, and resolves to make one decision entirely on her own.
Setting: A village in Surrey, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
