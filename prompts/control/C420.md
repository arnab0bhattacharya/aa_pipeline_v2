Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young man falsely accused of a crime committed by his father is tried and acquitted, but his reputation is ruined. His father, who could clear him, remains silent. The son must decide whether to expose his father or bear the shame himself.
Setting: An assize town in the north of England, 1820s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
