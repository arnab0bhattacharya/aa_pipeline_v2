Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young man who works in a government office and writes poetry in the evenings meets, at a lecture, a young woman who works for a charity. They argue about everything. They begin to meet every week to continue the argument.
Setting: London, 1910s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
