Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A family of daughters is thrown into consternation by the arrival of a letter from their father's long-estranged brother, who has made a fortune abroad and proposes to visit. No one remembers what the quarrel was about, and each sister has a different theory about what he will want.
Setting: A manor house in Somerset, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
