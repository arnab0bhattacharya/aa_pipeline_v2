Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young woman nursing her dying aunt through a long winter learns that her inheritance will go instead to a cousin who never visited. She is free and penniless at once, and must decide whether to accept a place offered by a family she knows to be cold.
Setting: An isolated farmhouse on the Yorkshire moors, 1830s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
