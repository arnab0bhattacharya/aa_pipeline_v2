Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young doctor in a manufacturing town treats the injuries of children who work in the mills. He presses the owners to shorten their hours and is told to mind his trade. A mill owner's daughter secretly gives him money for a dispensary.
Setting: A manufacturing town in Lancashire, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
