Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young man who has been engaged to a beautiful and conventional girl finds himself drawn to her plain and unconventional friend, who works for a living and laughs at him. He does not know how to stop.
Setting: London and a house in the country, 1910s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
