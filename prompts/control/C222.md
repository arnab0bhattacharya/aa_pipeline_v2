Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]

The guests include a bishop, an actress, an explorer, and a young poet who has written a scandalous book. By the second course, everyone has discovered a secret about everyone else.
Setting: A house in Grosvenor Square, London, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
