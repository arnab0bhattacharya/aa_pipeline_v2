Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A respectable stockbroker receives an anonymous letter informing him that he is the rightful heir to a Portuguese marquisate. He dismisses it, then cannot stop thinking about it. His wife, who has always wished for a title, begins ordering new stationery before he has replied.
Setting: A villa in Clapham and the City of London, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
