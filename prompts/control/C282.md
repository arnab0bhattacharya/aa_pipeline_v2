Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young heiress is warned by her guardian that every man who courts her wants only her money. She resolves to test her suitors by pretending to have lost her fortune. Only one remains, and he is the one she likes least.
Setting: A house in Bath, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
