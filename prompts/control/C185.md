Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young princess who has been told that she is the most beautiful girl in the world goes to the market in disguise to discover the truth. A blind woman who sells flowers tells her she sings beautifully, and the princess does not know whether to be pleased.
Setting: A market in a royal city

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
