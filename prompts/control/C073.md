Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An English bookseller travelling to France to collect a debt from a Paris printer finds the city in uproar and the printer arrested. The printer's young apprentice begs the bookseller to carry a parcel of papers back to England. The bookseller has never done a brave thing in his life.
Setting: Paris, 1792

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
