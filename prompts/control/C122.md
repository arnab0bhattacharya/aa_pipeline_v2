Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A little lame boy who lives in a cottage at the edge of a great forest is visited each night by a white stag. He follows it into the forest, and finds a city where no one is lame, no one is old, and no one is happy.
Setting: A great forest in a northern kingdom

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
