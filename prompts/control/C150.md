Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An English sea captain smuggling refugees out of France is asked to carry a child whose parents have been arrested. The child's nurse insists on coming. The captain suspects the nurse is a spy for the revolutionary committee.
Setting: A Channel port in France, 1793

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
