Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A mother whose ambition is to see her daughters married to titles takes them to a resort where a young lord is said to be staying. The lord proves to be an awkward boy of seventeen with a governess. The daughters must disguise their mother's disappointment from the whole resort.
Setting: A seaside resort in Sussex, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
