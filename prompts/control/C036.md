Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An English teacher at a girls' boarding school abroad is accused by a pupil of reading her private letters. The headmistress believes the pupil. The teacher, friendless in a foreign city and unable to argue fluently in its language, must defend her honour or lose her post.
Setting: A boarding school in a Flemish city, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
