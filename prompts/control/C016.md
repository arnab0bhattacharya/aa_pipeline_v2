Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A woman's life from childhood to old age is glimpsed in a series of moments: a summer at the seaside, a proposal in a garden, a death in a nursing home, a walk in the park with a grandchild. The moments do not quite make a story, and she wonders whether they make a life.
Setting: Cornwall, London and Sussex, 1880s to 1920s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
