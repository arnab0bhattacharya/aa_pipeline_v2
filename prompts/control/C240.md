Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A widowed baronet's second daughter is courted simultaneously by a rich, tedious neighbour and a clever but poor curate. Her elder sister, who has no suitors at all, gives her advice so evidently self-interested that the younger is driven to prefer whichever man her sister dislikes.
Setting: A manor house in Hampshire, 1800s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
