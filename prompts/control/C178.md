Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A family of gypsies is blamed when a village church's silver chalice disappears. The vicar doubts their guilt and writes to an investigator, mentioning that the churchwarden has recently paid off a large debt and that the bell-ringers swear they heard a horse at midnight.
Setting: A village in Worcestershire, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
