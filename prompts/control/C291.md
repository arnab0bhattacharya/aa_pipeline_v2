Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A governess overhears her employers discussing her as though she were furniture. That night their youngest child falls dangerously ill, and only the governess knows how to nurse him. She must decide whether to stay out of duty or leave out of self-respect.
Setting: A gentleman's house in Derbyshire, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
