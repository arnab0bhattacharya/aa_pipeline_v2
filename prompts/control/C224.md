Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young woman jilted on the eve of her wedding by a man she loved shuts herself away from the world. Her younger brother, a lively boy of fourteen, refuses to let her withdraw, and drags her out to the hills each morning.
Setting: A house in the Peak District, 1820s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
