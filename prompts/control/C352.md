Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An heiress who has refused every suitor is visited by a former tutor, now a poor schoolmaster, who once taught her brother. She finds that he is the only man who speaks to her without flattery, and that her fortune stands between them.
Setting: A manor in the West Riding, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
