Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A lady's maid is dismissed after her mistress's necklace disappears, though she swears she never touched it. Her brother, a police constable, privately doubts the official case and seeks help from an investigator, who notices that the necklace's insurance was increased only a week before.
Setting: A country house and London, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
