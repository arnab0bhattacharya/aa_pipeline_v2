Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
In a future England, a prophet arises who claims that the pestilence is a punishment and that only his followers will be spared. A young doctor who has seen the disease up close sets out to expose him, and finds the prophet sincere.
Setting: A town in the English Midlands, late in the twenty-first century

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
