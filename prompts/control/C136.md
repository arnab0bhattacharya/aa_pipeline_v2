Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A captain discovers that his chief officer has been secretly drinking, and that the ship's course has been wrongly reckoned for a week. Reporting the officer will ruin him. Not reporting him may lose the ship.
Setting: A steamer in the Red Sea, 1880s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
