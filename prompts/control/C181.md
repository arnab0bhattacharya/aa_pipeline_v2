Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A cheerful bicycling clerk on his annual holiday encounters a young woman who has run away from her guardian. He offers to escort her through the country lanes to her aunt's. Her guardian, in pursuit, believes the worst of both.
Setting: The lanes of Hampshire, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
