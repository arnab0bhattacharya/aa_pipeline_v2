Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A brewer's heir receives a series of telegrams, each announcing a death that has not yet occurred. The first two names belong to men who die within days. The third name is his own, and he comes to an investigator in a state of barely controlled fear.
Setting: London and a brewery town in Kent, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
