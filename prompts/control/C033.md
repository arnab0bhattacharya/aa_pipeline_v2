Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young woman who has nursed her mother through a long and terrible illness is left alone in the world at nineteen. A distant relative offers her a home. She refuses, and resolves to live by herself in her mother's cottage, though the village thinks her strange.
Setting: A village in Wales, 1820s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
