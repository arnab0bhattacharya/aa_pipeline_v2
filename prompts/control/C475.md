Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A wealthy manufacturer who rose from poverty by cheating his first employer is now a celebrated benefactor of his town. The employer's grandson, a young schoolmaster, has been invited to give the address at the opening of the benefactor's new school.
Setting: A town in Lancashire, 1830s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
