Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A retired naval captain who has never spoken of a mutiny he suppressed in his youth is asked by a young historian to describe it for a book. The captain refuses. The historian then produces a letter, written by a mutineer the captain hanged, that tells the story differently.
Setting: A house on the Kent coast, 1830s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
