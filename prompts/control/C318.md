Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A British consul in a small republic, who has spent twenty years writing reports nobody reads, receives a telegram announcing that a warship is coming to protect British interests. He realises he no longer knows what the British interests are, or whether he still shares them.
Setting: A South American port, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
