Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A shipwrecked crew in an open boat must decide whether to make for the nearest land, where natives are reported hostile, or a distant port. The captain, who is dying, will not decide. The mate and the bosun cannot agree.
Setting: An open boat in the Java Sea, 1880s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
