Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An elderly baronet is visited by a man claiming to be the son of a brother lost on a voyage to the Cape. The claimant knows family secrets, but the baronet's housekeeper insists the brother had no child. An investigator must test the claim before the will is changed.
Setting: A country house in Sussex, with a past at the Cape, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
