Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young man who believes himself destined to be a great poet takes a cheap lodging and waits for inspiration. His landlady, his creditors, and a sensible girl who sells gloves across the street all have opinions about his talent. None of them agrees with his.
Setting: A lodging house in the Borough, London, 1830s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
