Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A gang of boys who sleep under the arches of a railway viaduct adopt a lost little girl who cannot remember her own name. They feed her by begging and petty theft. When a well-dressed lady begins asking questions about the child in the neighbourhood, the boys must decide whether to give her up.
Setting: Railway arches in Bermondsey, London, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
