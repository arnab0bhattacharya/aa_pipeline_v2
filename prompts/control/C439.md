Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A young woman who has been engaged for two years to a man waiting for preferment begins to doubt that she loves him. He is good, dull, and devoted. She must decide whether to break the engagement and face her family's displeasure.
Setting: A village in Hampshire, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
