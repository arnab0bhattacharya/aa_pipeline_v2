Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
In a future Europe where nations have dwindled after a pestilence, an English physician sails to Lisbon in search of a remedy said to have been found there. The city he finds is calm, orderly, and unwilling to share what it knows.
Setting: Lisbon, late in the twenty-first century

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
