Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A farmer who has never been able to read is elected overseer of the poor. He must keep accounts he cannot check. His daughter offers to keep them for him, and soon discovers that the previous overseer stole from the poor rate.
Setting: A village in Leicestershire, 1810s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
