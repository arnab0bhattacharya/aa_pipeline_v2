Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A governess at a wealthy household is befriended by a visiting cousin of the family, an artist. He paints her portrait and tells her she has a remarkable face. She does not trust flattery, and suspects the portrait is meant to provoke someone else in the house.
Setting: A country house in Warwickshire, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
