Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
An undertaker's mute, hired to stand at funerals looking sorrowful, is mistaken for a grieving relative at a rich man's funeral, and invited to the reading of the will. He is too frightened to correct the mistake.
Setting: A churchyard and a house in Kensington, London, 1840s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
