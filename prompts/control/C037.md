Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A colonial official on a remote island discovers that the islanders have a medicine that reverses ageing. A company in London wants the formula. The official must decide whether to betray the islanders or his employers.
Setting: An island in the Pacific, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
