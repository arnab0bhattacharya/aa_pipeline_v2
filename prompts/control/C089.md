Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A dandy who is famous for never having been seen without a buttonhole loses his last flower on the morning of a great wedding. Every florist in London is closed. His pursuit of a single carnation leads him through the length and breadth of the city.
Setting: London on a Sunday morning, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
