Write an original piece of prose fiction.

[Scenario]
A pompous alderman who has organised a banquet to celebrate a new town hall discovers on the morning of the feast that the contractor has run off with the funds and the roof is unfinished. He must preside over the banquet in the rain without admitting anything is wrong.
Setting: A Midlands market town, 1850s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
