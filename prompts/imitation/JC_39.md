[Scenario]
A cabinet minister's private secretary is approached by an old school friend, now an anarchist, who asks for a meeting with the minister. The secretary agrees, believing he can bring about reconciliation. The friend has other intentions.
Setting: Westminster, London, 1890s

[Requirements]
- About 1,200 words of continuous narrative prose, in paragraphs.
- Invent all names of characters and places.
- No title, headings, notes or commentary before or after the story.
