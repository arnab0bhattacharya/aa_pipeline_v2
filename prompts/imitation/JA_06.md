Write an original piece of prose fiction in the style of Jane Austen (1775–1817).

[Notes on the author's manner (for orientation, not a checklist)]
Poised, ironic narration, closely attuned to characters' thoughts and slipping into their view with gentle mockery. Elegant, balanced sentences prefer understatement to emphasis and often turn on a quiet antithesis. An abstract vocabulary of judgement, manners, feeling and conduct, with sparse physical description. Conversation is a contest of social position, revealing folly and good sense in what each speaker chooses to say.
