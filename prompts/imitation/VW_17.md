[Scenario]
A young woman from a family of lawyers is expected to marry her father's most promising pupil. He is earnest, admiring, and rather dull. She accepts him because there is no reason not to, and spends the engagement looking for a reason.
Setting: Lincoln's Inn and a house in Kensington, London, 1910s
