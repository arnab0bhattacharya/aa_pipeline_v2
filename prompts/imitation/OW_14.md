An urbane, detached voice that delights in paradox and the inversion of moral commonplaces. Polished, balanced sentences often turn on an epigrammatic reversal; in fable, a cadenced, solemn simplicity. Talk sparkles with aphorism and repartee; characters say outrageous things with perfect composure.
