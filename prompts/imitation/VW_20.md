Sinuous sentences interrupt themselves, turn aside and pass between minds; rhythm matters more than plot. Impressionistic imagery of light, colour, sound and passing time, with ordinary objects charged with significance. Spoken words are sparse and fragmentary, set against the much larger life of unspoken thought.
