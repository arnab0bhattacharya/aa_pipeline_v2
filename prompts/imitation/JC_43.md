Long, rhythmic sentences layered with qualification, often rendering an impression before its meaning is understood. Dense atmospheric description of light, darkness, heat, water and silence, beside abstract moral vocabulary. Speech is halting, oblique or formal, with foreign idioms and much left unsaid.
